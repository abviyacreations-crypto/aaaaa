About Me

Design is how I

I'm a graphic designer based in Mumbai with an obsessive eye for what works — visually, strategically, and emotionally. Currently at Futuready Media, where I own the visual language of the brand day-to-day.

I believe great design is the difference between being forgotten and being remembered.
